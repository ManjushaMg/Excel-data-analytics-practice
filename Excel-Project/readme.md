Excel Project: Healthcare Data Analysis and Insights  

This project analyse the healthcare datset and find out insights from patient health profiles,medical histories.It explores the distribution of cancer history among smokers and non-smokers,compares total number of major surgeries and average HbA1C differ between patients with and without a history of transplants,investigate healthcare charges vary based on different weight statuses and diabetes statuses , compare the average charges for each hospital tier within different states and finding out correlation between age and both BMI and HbA1C in the dataset if present and Explores the relationship between age and healthcare charges.
Tool used:MS Excel, PowerQuery
Dataset Download:

2.Data Transformation
3.Data Exploration, Analysis & Visualization
4.Dashboard creation:added slicers

Insights
1.Hospital charge is high among those with diabetics than prediabetic patients
2.Obese patients weigh the high hospital charge
3.Tier 2 city have high hospital charge
